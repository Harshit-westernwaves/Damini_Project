1. Project Purpose & Executive Summary

The AI Student Performance & Academic Guidance System is a specialized decision-support platform designed to address the pervasive challenge of late-stage academic intervention in higher engineering education. In traditional institutional frameworks, academic distress is typically discovered only after semester examination results are published—by which point remedial counseling is often too late to prevent GPA decline or course backlogs.

This system bridges this critical operational gap by establishing a continuous telemetry loop that transforms leading indicators (daily coursework study hours, lecture attendance rates, baseline previous semester marks, and continuous internal assignment scores) into probabilistic forecasts and personalized, rule-based academic guidance plans.

A. Data Preparation & Statistical Profiling

  • Standardized Ingestion: Enforce strict adherence to the 6 canonical columns: Student ID, Study hrs, Attendance, Previous marks, Assignment scores, and Final performance.
  • Parametric Cleaning: Provide targeted imputation (Mean/Median for continuous scores, Mode for categorical IDs) and deduplication.
  • Exploratory Profiling: Compute five-number summaries, standard deviation dispersion, and multi-variable Pearson correlation matrices.

B. Predictive Modeling & Guidance Action Plans

  • Supervised Regression & Classification: Fit Linear Regression (R² = 0.856), Logistic Regression, and Random Forest pipelines with 80/20 train/test splitting.
  • Early Warning Alerts: Enforce automated flags whenever attendance drops below the 75% institutional examination gate.
  • Conversational LLM Advising: Integrate hosted Groq Llama-3.3-70B models for conversational study scheduling and remedial guidance.

Powers Ordinary Least Squares Linear Regression, multi-class Logistic Regression, and Random Forest Regressors. Includes StandardScaler normalization and Joblib persistence.

Groq Llama-3 LLM Inference

Ultra-low-latency conversational AI engine running llama-3.3-70b-versatile for dynamic educational advising, backed by offline rule-based heuristics.

Tailwind CSS Design System

Engineered with the Focus & Flow academic theme: low-eye-strain slate-50 base layout, Indigo primary action colors, Emerald data highlights, and Amber warning accents.

Pandas / NumPy Data Analytics

High-performance vectorized dataframe operations for CSV ingestion, schema validation, quantile profiling, and synthetic multi-cohort calibration.
