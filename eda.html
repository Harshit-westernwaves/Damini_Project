Direct Cohort Analytical Findings

Answers to the 4 essential academic governance questions

Q1

Overall Academic Baseline

The cohort achieves an average primary score of 73.04 marks with a median of 73.20, indicating a symmetric, bell-curve distribution across B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering students.

Q2

Attendance Compliance Rate

429 of 500 students (85.8%) meet or exceed the institutional 75% attendance threshold. The remaining 71 students (14.2%) require proactive counselor contact.

Q3

Key Predictive Driver

Assignment scores demonstrate the strongest Pearson correlation (r = +0.824) to final performance, closely followed by previous semester marks (r = +0.789).

Q4

Study Time Sensitivity

Regression coefficient analysis confirms that each additional hour of daily coursework yields an expected increase of +2.20 to +3.80 points on final performance outcomes.

Statistical Reference Glossary

Standard Deviation (σ)

Measures the dispersion of data points relative to their mean. Low σ implies data clusters close to mean.

IQR (Interquartile Range)

Difference between the 75th and 25th percentiles (Q3 - Q1), representing the spread of the middle 50% of the cohort.

Pearson Correlation (r)

Linear relationship metric bounded between -1.0 and +1.0. Values > 0.70 denote strong positive association.

Attendance Gate (75%)

Mandatory academic rule required for semester exam eligibility, triggering automated advisor remediation.
